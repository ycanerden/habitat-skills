# Follow-Up Tracks

You are helping a founder handle every scenario that happens after first contact. Most deals are lost not because of a bad product — but because of no follow-up, bad timing, or wrong messaging at the wrong moment.

## Before Starting

**Check for context first:**
If `.claude/founder-context.md` exists, read it. Use ICP, product, and pricing context.

If no context, gather:
- What's the product and what does it do?
- What happened so far with this lead? (intro email, demo call, etc.)
- What was their response or reaction?
- What track are we in? (warm / ghosted / objection / post-demo)
- What's the next action you want them to take?

## The 4 Follow-Up Tracks

---

## Track 1: Warm Lead

**Situation:** They replied positively ("sounds interesting," "would love to learn more," booked a call), but no conversion yet.

**Goal:** Move them to next step (demo, trial, or paid).

### Day 0 — Confirmation + Pre-Call Value

Send immediately when a call is booked.

```
Subject: Looking forward to talking, [Name] — quick context

Hi [Name],

Looking forward to our call on [day/time].

To make the most of our 20 minutes, here's what I'd love to cover:
- [Pain point they mentioned or likely have]
- How [one or two customers] solved it
- Whether it makes sense for [their company]

If anything changes before then, just let me know.

[Your name]
```

### Day 0 — Pre-Call Prep Checklist (internal, not sent)

```
□ Research: [Name]'s LinkedIn, company, recent posts
□ Look for: company size, tech stack signals, recent hires, news
□ Review: any previous emails or context
□ Prepare: 1 relevant case study or result to mention
□ Confirm: calendar invite sent with video link
□ Plan: first question to ask in the call
```

### Same Day (after the call) — Post-Demo Follow-Up

Send within 2 hours.

```
Subject: Notes from our call + next step

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the time — I really appreciated the candid conversation.

From what I heard:
- [Pain point they mentioned]
- [Their current situation or workaround]
- [What they said they wanted]

Based on that, the next step that makes sense is [specific action — trial, pricing proposal, intro to a customer, etc.].

I've attached [relevant resource / pricing / next step doc].

Does [day/time] work to reconnect after you've had a chance to try it / review it?

[Your name]
```

### Day 3 — Value Add

If they haven't responded to the post-demo email.

```
Subject: One thing I forgot to mention

Hi [Name],

One thing I didn't cover in our call — [relevant feature / case study / insight they'd care about].

[1–2 sentences on why it's relevant to their situation.]

Thought it was worth sharing. Let me know if you have questions.

[Your name]
```

### Day 7 — Gentle Nudge

```
Subject: Still thinking it over?

Hi [Name],

Circling back in case the timing got away from you.

Happy to [answer questions / adjust the proposal / set up a trial] — whatever makes it easier to move forward.

Worth a quick reply?

[Your name]
```

## Track 2: Ghosted

**Situation:** They went quiet after initial contact or after a positive first reply.

**Goal:** Get any reply — yes, no, or "not now."

### Rule: Never chase more than 4 times. Respect their time.

### Day 3 — First Follow-Up (value add)

Don't mention that they haven't replied. Add something new.

```
Subject: Thought this might be relevant

Hi [Name],

I wanted to share [relevant resource / customer story / insight] — thought it might be useful given [their context].

[1–2 sentences on the relevant insight.]

If the timing isn't right, just let me know — happy to reconnect when it makes more sense.

[Your name]
```

### Day 7 — Second Follow-Up (soft check-in)

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Just checking in — did the original message land in a busy week?

[One sentence re-stating the core value prop.]

No pressure — a quick yes or no is helpful either way.

[Your name]
```

### Day 14 — Third Follow-Up (shift the frame)

Try a different angle — different hook or problem.

```
Subject: Different angle

Hi [Name],

I reached out last week about [original topic] — I might have led with the wrong angle.

The reason I think [their company] is a good fit is specifically [different reason / more targeted problem].

[One sentence on specific relevance.]

Still worth 15 minutes?

[Your name]
```

### Day 21 — Breakup Email (highest reply rate)

This often gets the highest reply rate of the entire sequence.

```
Subject: Closing the loop

Hi [Name],

I've reached out a few times and haven't heard back — I'll take that as a signal it's not the right time.

I'll stop following up after this. If the situation changes, you can always reach me at [email].

Either way, good luck with [something specific about their work].

[Your name]
```

**Why the breakup email works:** It removes pressure. People feel safe replying to end the loop. You'll often get "sorry, been slammed — let's actually talk" replies.

## Track 3: Objection

**Situation:** They replied but raised a concern. Common objections:

| Objection | Translation | Counter |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| "Too expensive" | Unclear ROI | Make the value tangible in their terms |
| "Not the right time" | Low urgency | Understand what changes the timing |
| "We already have [X]" | Incumbent solution | Find the gap the incumbent doesn't address |
| "I need to check with my team" | Not the decision maker | Get access to the real decision maker |
| "We're not ready for this" | Unclear fit | Understand what "ready" looks like |
| "Send me more info" | Not convinced enough to talk | Make the next step lower friction |

### Day 0 — Acknowledge + Reframe

Don't argue. Validate, then shift.

**"Too expensive":**
```
Hi [Name],

Fair point — I hear that often.

Can I ask: is it the total price, or the certainty of getting [outcome] that's the concern?

If it's the latter, I'd love to show you specifically how [similar customer] got [ROI result] within [timeframe]. That might make it easier to evaluate whether the numbers work.

Open to a 15-minute call to look at the specifics?

[Your name]
```

**"Not the right time":**
```
Hi [Name],

Totally understand — timing matters.

Can I ask what would need to change to make this the right time? Is it [budget cycle / team capacity / a specific milestone]?

I ask because I'd rather reach out when it makes sense for you than keep pinging at the wrong moment.

[Your name]
```

**"We already have [competitor]":**
```
Hi [Name],

Makes sense — [competitor] is solid for [what they're good at].

The founders I talk to who use [competitor] usually still struggle with [specific gap]. Is that true for you?

If it is, that's exactly where [product] fills in. Worth 15 minutes to see if there's a real gap?

[Your name]
```

**"Send me more info":**

Don't send a deck. Make it a conversation instead.

```
Hi [Name],

Happy to share more — but I find a 15-minute call is 10x more useful than a doc, because I can tailor what's relevant to your situation.

What specifically would you want to understand better? I'll make sure we cover that in the call.

[Your name]
```

### Day 5 — Follow-Up on Objection

If they didn't reply to your reframe:

```
Hi [Name],

Just following up on my last message about [their objection].

Happy to address [specific concern] differently — or if the timing just isn't right, that's okay too.

What would make it easier to move forward?

[Your name]
```

## Track 4: Post-Demo — Deal Closing

**Situation:** You've had a demo or discovery call. They're interested. Now close.

### Same Day (within 2 hours)

Covered in Track 1 above. Key: reflect what they said, name the next step clearly.

### Day 3 — Check-In

```
Subject: Checking in — any questions after our call?

Hi [Name],

Checking in to see if you had a chance to think about [next step from call].

Happy to [answer questions / adjust anything / set up a trial] — whatever helps.

[Your name]
```

### Day 7 — Social Proof Drop

Share a relevant story without being pushy.

```
Subject: Quick story you might find relevant

Hi [Name],

I was thinking of our conversation when I saw this — [customer name] was in a similar situation to [their situation] and got [result] in [timeframe].

Thought it might be useful context as you think it over.

[Your name]
```

### Day 14 — Urgency / Decision

Don't manufacture fake urgency. Use real reasons.

```
Subject: Checking timeline on your end

Hi [Name],

I want to be respectful of your time — I know decisions like this take a few rounds internally.

Is there a timeline on your end? [We're onboarding new accounts in the first week of the month / I want to make sure we can hold a spot / etc.]

Let me know if there's anything I can do to help move it forward on your side.

[Your name]
```

## Principles

- **Follow up more than feels comfortable.** Most founders give up after 1–2 messages. 80% of sales happen after 5+ touchpoints.
- **Each touchpoint must add value.** Don't just say "checking in." Bring something new: a resource, a story, a question.
- **Make it easy to say no.** Permission to say no reduces friction. "If the timing's off, just let me know" often gets a yes.
- **Objections are openings.** A reply — any reply — means they're thinking about it. That's better than silence.
- **Personalize to the moment.** Reference what they said in the call. Reference what's happening at their company. Prove you were listening.
- **Stop at 4 follow-ups.** After that, add them to a nurture list and revisit in 90 days.
